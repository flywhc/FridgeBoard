# Third-party icon assets

The PNG files under `icons/skeuomorphic/` are adapted copies of the 3D assets from
[Microsoft Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), pinned to commit
`62ecdc0d7ca5c6df32148c169556bc8d3782fca4`.

They are distributed under the MIT License. The original copyright notice and license are
included in `FLUENT_EMOJI_LICENSE.txt`.

The files are bundled as application assets and are not exposed as a standalone icon pack.
