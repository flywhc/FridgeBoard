# Third-party icon assets

The PNG files under `icons/skeuomorphic/` are downloaded from individual icon
pages in the [Thiings collection](https://www.thiings.co/things). The exact
object-to-logic-key mapping and original image URLs are recorded in
`THIINGS_ASSET_SOURCES.md`.

The files are bundled as application assets and are not exposed as a standalone
icon pack. Before distributing a commercial build, review the current Thiings
[terms](https://www.thiings.co/terms), [about page](https://www.thiings.co/about),
and [pricing/licensing page](https://www.thiings.co/pricing) for the applicable
license and attribution requirements.
